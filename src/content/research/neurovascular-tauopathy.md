title: "Neurovascular pathways driving tauopathy"
order: 20
summary: "Dysregulation of the brain's vascular tree is increasingly recognized as a major contributor to the pathogenesis of Alzheimer's disease. The lab uses a viral transcriptional profiling toolbox to capture early gliovascular dysfunction during prodromal stages of tauopathy and to identify druggable mediators — most notably Mark4, a microtubule-associated enzyme that potentiates tau aggregation after ischemic axonal injury."
---

This program is supported by the NIA and the Ressler Foundation. Key findings include the
discovery that ischemic axonal injury up-regulates MARK4 in cortical neurons and primes tau
phosphorylation and aggregation (Acta Neuropathol Commun 2019), and that miR-142-3p regulates
cortical oligodendrocyte co-expression networks tied to tauopathy (Hum Mol Genet 2021). We are
now testing first-in-class Mark4 inhibitors in mouse models of mixed vascular-AD dementia.
