---
title: "Biomarkers for vascular cognitive impairment"
order: 40
summary: "Damage to the brain's small blood vessels by chronic inflammation increases the risk of dementia and stroke. The lab has identified plasma protein signatures — including an IL-18-centered inflammatory network and placental growth factor (PlGF) — that track MRI-defined vascular brain injury and predict longitudinal cognitive change."
---

These efforts are conducted through the NIH-funded MarkVCID and MarkVCID2 consortia and
collaborations with the Diverse VCID Study. Recent work shows that plasma PlGF and white-matter
free water mediate associations between vascular pathology and cognition (Alzheimers Dement 2023,
2025), and that an IL-18-centered network biomarker composite is associated with incident stroke
risk (Stroke 2024).
