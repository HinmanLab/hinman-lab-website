title: "Intracranial atherosclerotic disease"
order: 30
summary: "Intracranial atherosclerotic disease (ICAD) is the leading worldwide cause of ischemic stroke. In partnership with Drs. Liebeskind and Kaneko, the lab studies how patient-specific shear stress patterns drive endothelial pathophysiology and localized thrombosis, using novel 3D-printed flow models and spatial transcriptomic profiling of human cerebrovascular tissue."
---

We have developed flow-dependent endothelialized 3D models of ICAD lesions (Transl Stroke Res 2025)
and characterized endothelial shear-stress signatures and platelet Fc&gamma;RIIa expression in
human disease (Front Neurol 2021). The goal is to identify mechanistically defined subtypes of
ICAD that respond differently to medical vs. endovascular therapy.
