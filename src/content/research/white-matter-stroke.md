title: "White matter stroke and axonal repair"
order: 10
summary: "Subcortical white matter strokes are common, under-recognized, and a leading driver of vascular cognitive impairment. The lab studies how oligodendrocyte–axon–vascular cell crosstalk fails after white matter injury, and identifies pathways — including SARM1-mediated axonal degeneration and Nogo-receptor signaling — that can be therapeutically engaged to promote remyelination and circuit recovery."
---

Our work has defined molecular reorganization at the axon initial segment and node of Ranvier
after focal cortical and white matter stroke (Stroke 2013; Brain 2015) and established versatile
murine models of subcortical white matter stroke (J Vis Exp 2016). Current projects target
SARM1-mediated axonal degeneration in ischemic stroke and test small-molecule inhibitors of
this pathway as candidate post-stroke neuroprotectants.
