Russell is a PhD candidate in Biochemistry, Molecular and Structural Biology at UCLA. He earned his dual B.S./M.S. in Biochemistry from UCLA in 2023 and joined the Hinman Lab as a Staff Research Associate II in April 2024 before beginning his PhD. His research uses patient-specific 3D cerebrovascular models, confocal imaging, and single-cell multiomic approaches to investigate how disturbed blood flow regulates endothelial biology in intracranial atherosclerotic disease and stroke. Outside the lab, Russell enjoys reading, running, and gaming.
