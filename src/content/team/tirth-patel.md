Tirth Patel completed his MD/PhD at Washington University in St. Louis in 2020. He completed adult neurology residency and fellowship in neurobehavior at UCLA. While in the lab, he completed postdoctoral research on the role of meningeal lymphatics in tauopathy as part of his NINDS R25/UE5 Fellowship. He is now Assistant Professor of Neurology at UCLA.
