Sihwan completed his PhD at Chung-Ang University in Seoul, South Korea, where he analyzed brain
transcriptomes of Down syndrome patients and stressed mice using weighted gene co-expression
network analysis (WGCNA). He joined the lab in October 2023 to study molecular mechanisms of
neurovascular contributions in Alzheimer's disease.
