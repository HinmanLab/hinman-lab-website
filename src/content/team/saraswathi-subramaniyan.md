Saraswathi received her PhD in Neuroscience from the Medical University of Vienna in 2016. She joined the lab in fall 2025 and focuses on the convergence of ischemic stroke and neurodegeneration, working to understand the mechanisms that orchestrate white matter injury and tauopathy. Outside the lab, her days are filled with family, Tamizh music, reading, hiking, and traveling.
