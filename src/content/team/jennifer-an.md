Jenny is an NSIDP graduate student in the Hinman Lab. She received her B.S. in Biomedical Sciences with a minor in Biomedical Physics from the University of South Florida, and built a foundation in molecular biology as a research technician in labs at Dartmouth College studying the cancer biology of colorectal tumors and gliomas. Her passion for translational and regenerative neuroscience led her to the Hinman Lab, where she studies the molecular mechanisms that activate axon regeneration in stroke-injured neurons to promote pro-growth pathways for functional motor recovery — work she hopes will help broaden the recovery window for stroke patients. Outside of lab, Jenny enjoys roller skating, running, climbing, and backpacking to go off grid and recharge.
