Katherine Mun completed her vascular neurology residency at UCLA in 2022. While in the lab, she served as the site PI for the DISCOVERY Network study at UCLA and published on the role of inflammation and biomarkers in stroke. She is presently an Assistant Professor of Neurology at the University of Buffalo.
