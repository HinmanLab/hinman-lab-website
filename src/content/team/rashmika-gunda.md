Rashmika is a Staff Research Associate in the Hinman Lab. She holds a Master of Science in Biological Sciences from Purdue University Fort Wayne and joined the lab in January 2024. Her work focuses on quantifying blood-based biomarkers of cerebral small vessel disease and vascular cognitive impairment in human serum and plasma using a range of immunoassay platforms. She aspires to build a career in the biotech industry. Outside of work, she loves exploring coffee shops, dancing, and traveling to scenic destinations.
