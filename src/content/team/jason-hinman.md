Jason is a physician-scientist, trained in vascular neurology and neurorehabilitation, with clinical expertise in stroke, stroke recovery, vascular cognitive impairment, and dementia. In July 2026, he was appointed the Susan & David Wilstein Endowed Chair in Medicine at the David Geffen School of Medicine at UCLA. In addition to heading the UCLA VCID CWOW and his own lab, he also serves as Director of the Katherine and Benjamin Kagan AD Treatment Program and Director of the Easton Center Labs for Neurodegeneration. He co-directs the UCLA Neuroscience Physician-Scientist Training Program. He believes in fairness in education, science, and medicine.
